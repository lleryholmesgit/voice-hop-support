Voice Hop

Your voice becomes a jumping adventure.

Record 5–10 seconds, choose a companion, and tap to jump while your original audio plays. Higher notes create higher terrain. Pauses create safe zones.

Gameplay

This gameplay recording is silent.

Quick help

  • Recording: tap Record your voice and allow microphone access. Use speech or humming, changing your pitch gently. Avoid clipping or loud background noise.
  • No microphone permission? Open iOS Settings → Apps → Voice Hop → Microphone. You can still play a challenge received from a friend without recording.
  • No sound? Turn up media volume and check whether audio is going to headphones or Bluetooth speakers.
  • Sharing: share the .vchal challenge file using the iOS share sheet. The recipient needs Voice Hop installed and opens the file with Voice Hop. A replay video is a video, not a playable challenge.
  • File will not open? Use the original .vchal file exported by Voice Hop. Files that are damaged, too large, or incompatible are rejected. Ask the sender to export again.
  • Delete a challenge: touch and hold it in your list, then select Delete. Deleting the app removes local app data; device backups are controlled separately in Apple settings.

Contact support

Email ydengeh@yahoo.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or private recordings. Only send audio if you choose to and have permission from anyone recorded.

Ayuda en español

Graba entre 5 y 10 segundos, elige un personaje y toca para saltar mientras escuchas la grabación original. Los tonos agudos crean terreno más alto y las pausas crean zonas seguras.

Para activar el micrófono, abre Ajustes de iOS → Apps → Voice Hop → Micrófono. Si no oyes sonido, comprueba el volumen y la salida Bluetooth. Comparte el archivo .vchal original: el destinatario necesita tener Voice Hop instalado. Mantén pulsado un reto para eliminarlo.

Soporte: ydengeh@yahoo.com. Incluye modelo, versión de iOS y descripción del problema. No envíes contraseñas, datos de pago ni grabaciones privadas. La primera versión es gratuita, sin compras integradas ni anuncios. Política de privacidad.
