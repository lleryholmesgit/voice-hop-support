QUESTIONS

How does Voice Hop work?

Is this a voice-controlled jumping game?

Your voice creates the level first; it does not steer the character live. During play, you tap or hold to jump while hearing the original recording.

Do I need the microphone to play?

No. Microphone access is needed to record a new challenge. You can play a .vchal challenge received from a friend without recording.

How do I send a playable level?

Share the original .vchal challenge file through the iOS share sheet. The recipient needs Voice Hop installed. A replay video is for watching, not playing.

Is the recording uploaded?

Recording and level generation happen on your device. A recording leaves the device only when you choose to share it through another service.
