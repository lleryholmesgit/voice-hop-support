QUESTIONS

How does Voice Hop work?

Is this a voice-controlled jumping game?

Your voice creates the level first; it does not steer the character live. During play, you tap or hold to jump while hearing the original recording.

Do I need the microphone to play?

No. Microphone access is needed to record a new challenge. You can play a challenge received from a friend without recording.

How do I send a playable level?

Choose Create link and share to upload the challenge to iCloud and send its link. The recipient needs Voice Hop and an active iCloud account. If the app is not installed, install it, return to the message, and tap the same link again; installation does not import it automatically. As a backup, send the original ZIP by email, save it to Files, and import it from Voice Hop. A replay video is for watching, not playing.

Is the recording uploaded?

Recording and level generation happen on your device. Creating an iCloud link uploads the original recording, generated level, display name, and caption to your private iCloud database. Anyone with the read-only link and an active iCloud account can receive, save, or forward the challenge. Other sharing services receive the files you choose to send. See our privacy policy for details.

How long does a challenge link last?

Voice Hop allows new imports for 30 days. Cloud cleanup is attempted when the sender next opens the app online in the same iCloud account, not at an exact deadline. In Manage cloud shares, the sender can request deletion and stop sharing; deletion is complete only after iCloud confirms it. Local originals and recipients' saved copies are not erased by that action.
